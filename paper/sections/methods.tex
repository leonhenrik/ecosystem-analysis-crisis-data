\section{Data and Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Data Source}
The dataset is exported from the Crisis Data Funding Compass, maintained by the Complex
Risk Analytics Fund (CRAF'd, \url{https://crafd.io}). It comprises two tables: organizations
(providers of crisis data products/services) and assets (specific data products/projects),
including supporting donors and investment types/themes. See
\href{https://github.com/}{data/README.md} in the code repository for the full data
dictionary and provenance notes.
